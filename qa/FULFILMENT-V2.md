# Postal fulfilment corrections — September 10, 2026

The production preparation now resolves the audit's paid-quantity, final-unit history, immutable printing, correction, print-volume and queue-pagination findings.

- Paid shortages cannot proceed on the operator's checkbox alone. The operator either restores the full ordered quantity from the original addresses or requests an authenticated buyer decision for the exact prepared selection and unchanged CAD total. The buyer can accept or refuse. Consent stores buyer id, terms version, counts, amount, selected-address fingerprint and timestamp. Refusal keeps production held; it is never described as a refund. Reimport or changed selection requires fresh matching consent.
- The order stores its historical-exclusion policy. Final civic/unit identities are checked again during import and approval, excluding the current campaign. The operator sees the prior date/order and cannot select blocked units. A short expiring per-broker database lease serializes approvals; the final save atomically requires/renews ownership. CAS also protects simultaneous buyer decisions, corrections, print generation and deposit declarations.
- Checkout freezes a compact rendered letter snapshot. Approved production uses that snapshot, so broker/profile/text/template edits do not change the print output. Old preparations without a snapshot capture one at their next approved print. Recipient substitutions are escaped, and each QR remains tied to its exact campaign and recipient.
- Each print file has at most 100 recipients. The admin has explicit batch cards and previous/next navigation; generation dates/counts/revision are stored. Opening a file is labeled generation, not proof of physical printing.
- Before deposit, an operator can reopen with a reason and confirmation that superseded paper will be destroyed. The old preparation and print batch record remain in the audit history, the campaign token rotates immediately, old QR links stop working, and a new import/review is required. Unchanged reprints preserve existing recipient codes.
- Deposit requires the current revision, exact prepared quantity, actual date/time, acknowledgement and all generated batches. Deposited campaigns cannot reopen. Paid or deposited orders cannot be silently cancelled; pending cancellation delegates to verified provider reconciliation.
- The operator queue has 50-item pages and total/page navigation. Existing payment/tax totals and original ordered addresses remain immutable during preparation. The operating promise remains 72 elapsed hours including weekends; holds are visible and require operator follow-up on delay.
